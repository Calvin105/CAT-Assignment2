# Project: Aetheria - High-Altitude Wind Energy Systems

## Overview
Aetheria is a pioneering technology company focused on harvesting high-altitude wind energy. Our mission is to provide consistent, clean, and low-cost renewable energy to the world by tapping into the powerful winds found above traditional turbine heights.

The website must convey a sense of **professionalism, futuristic innovation, and environmental stewardship**. It should feel "premium" and "engineered".

## Technical Scope
- **Framework**: React (using Vite)
- **Language**: JavaScript (ES6+) - **NO TypeScript**
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS (v4)
- **Icons**: Lucide React (for consistent, professional iconography)
- **UI/Animations**: React Bits (for high-quality, "wow" factor animations)
- **Routing**: React Router
- **Animation**: Framer Motion (as needed for React Bits or custom transitions)

## Design Guidelines
- **Aesthetic**: Clean, airy, modern. Use of whitespace, thin lines, and high-quality imagery.
- **Visual Hierarchy & Typography**:
    - **Primary Headings (H1)**: Large, bold, distinct color (e.g., Slate Gray or Deep Sky Blue). Use for page titles and hero statements.
    - **Secondary Headings (H2/H3)**: Medium weight, slightly smaller. Use for section titles.
    - **Body Text**: Legible, comfortable line height (1.6), neutral color (Slate Gray).
    - **Call to Action (CTA)**: High contrast (Energetic Teal), bold weight, clear hover states.
- **Iconography (Lucide React)**:
    - Use consistent stroke width (1.5px or 2px).
    - Icons should accompany text to enhance meaning, not replace it (unless standard UI controls).
    - Color icons to match the text hierarchy (e.g., primary color for feature icons, neutral for metadata).
- **Animation & Interactivity (React Bits)**:
    - Use React Bits components for high-impact visual elements (e.g., Hero backgrounds, interactive cards).
    - Ensure animations are smooth and do not distract from the content.
    - Use subtle hover effects on interactive elements to encourage engagement.
- **Color Palette**: Deep Sky Blue, Cloud White, Slate Gray, with accents of Energetic Teal.
- **Typography**: Sans-serif, geometric fonts (e.g., Inter, Roboto, or similar).
- **Media**: The site must be rich in visuals. Background videos of clouds/sky, renders of airborne turbines.

## Pages & Content

### 1. Landing Page (`/`)
- **Hero Section**: Full-screen video background (sky/clouds). Headline: "Energy from the Stratosphere." Subheadline: "Unlocking the limitless potential of high-altitude winds."
- **Introduction**: Brief overview of the technology (Airborne Wind Energy Systems - AWES).
- **Key Benefits**:
    - Higher Consistency
    - Lower Material Cost
    - Minimal Visual Impact
- **Call to Action**: "Partner with Us" or "Learn More".

### 2. Mission (`/mission`)
- **Focus**: The "Why".
- **Content**:
    - The Climate Challenge: Addressing the need for 24/7 renewable energy.
    - Our Commitment: Zero-carbon footprint manufacturing.
    - Sustainability Goals: 100% recyclable materials.
- **Visuals**: Nature-focused imagery, clean energy graphs.

### 3. Vision (`/vision`)
- **Focus**: The "Future".
- **Content**:
    - Global Energy Grid: How Aetheria fits into the future mix.
    - Technology Roadmap: From kW to MW scale systems.
    - A World Powered by Wind: Conceptual renders of future cities.
- **Visuals**: Futuristic renders, maps, blueprints.

### 4. Our Team (`/team`)
- **Focus**: Credibility and Expertise.
- **Content**:
    - Leadership bios (CEO, CTO, Chief Scientist).
    - Advisory Board (Industry veterans).
    - "Join Us" section.
- **Visuals**: Professional headshots, team working in R&D lab.
